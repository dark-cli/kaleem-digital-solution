The Kaleem logo

One mark, two lockups, three colours. This sheet replaces every earlier version. Use the master files; never redraw the logo by hand.

01

The mark

The Open Box: a square frame, open at its top-right corner, and one yellow block leaving through the gap. Data getting out of a closed system.

One ruleEach part is the one before it divided by φ. On a 110-unit grid they are Fibonacci numbers: 89, 55, 34, 21, 13.
Box89 × 89, walls 13 thick. Each open edge is 55 of wall and 34 of door: the golden section.
Block34 × 34, as wide as the door, logo yellow, no outline. Stepped out 21, so its back edge sits on the walls' inner face: on the threshold.
SymmetryMirrored across the diagonal through the door. The whole mark is a 110 × 110 square. One weight at every size. Full study: the construction board.

Mark construction · φ

MARK CONSTRUCTION · ADOPTED OCTOBER 2026

Out of the box, by the numbers

Others put you in a box. We walk you out of it. The frame is the box, the open corner is the door, the yellow block is you, mid-step. Every size below comes from one rule: each part is the one before it divided by φ. On a grid of 110 those parts are Fibonacci numbers.

PARTGRIDOF FRAMEφ
Frame8911
Wall run550.618φ⁻¹
Door and block340.382φ⁻²
Step out (and the gap)210.236φ⁻³
Wall thickness130.146φ⁻⁴

The wall is cut at the golden section. Each open edge is 55 of wall and 34 of door: 0.618 to 0.382, the classic division of a line.

The block is exactly as wide as the door. You fit through the opening you were given, no more, no less.

It stands on the threshold. The block has stepped out 21, so 13 of it is still inside, exactly the wall's thickness: its back edge lines up with the inner face of both walls (34 − 21 = 13). Out, but only just: the moment of leaving.

Mirror it across the diagonal and nothing changes. Both gaps are 21, both wall runs 55, and the block's centre sits on the axis.

The whole mark is a square, 110 × 110 (89 + 21), so it sits on the lockup's square without padding tricks.

One weight, every size

A 13/89 wall is heavy enough for a favicon and still calm on a sign. At 16px the wall is 1.9px, the gap 3px and the block 5px: still a box with someone leaving it. No separate bold or light version is needed.
