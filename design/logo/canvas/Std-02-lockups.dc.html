Lockups

Square 1 : name area φ (1.618), so every lockup is 2.618 : 1. The name area has two equal rows; "kaleem" and «كليم» fill the same width (21 kashidas after the ي). Each lockup has an Arabic version for Arabic pages: the mirror layout, mark on the right and «كليم» on top. The mark itself never flips.

E · Everyday lockup

On the mark’s own grid: 288 × 110 (110 × φ²). For everything else: site header and footer, business cards, social posts, invoices, email signatures.
