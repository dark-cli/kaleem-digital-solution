# Logos

The mark from the logo standard (repo: `design/logo/`): a box open at its top-right corner, with one logo-yellow block (`logo-block`, #f8d12f) stepping out through the door. Built on a 110-unit grid; the names in the lockups are outlined, so no font is needed.

- `kaleem-mark.svg` — on paper and pastels: box in `ink` (#1f1f1f).
- `kaleem-mark-chalk.svg` — on the night-sky `board`: box in `chalk` (#eceee6).
- `kaleem-mark-one-colour-ink.svg` / `-chalk.svg` — single-ink print: stamps, engraving.
- `kaleem-app-icon.svg` — app icon: night-sky tile, mark at 65%, centred on the grid point (50, 60).
- `kaleem-app-icon-white.svg` — the app icon on white.
- `kaleem-avatar.svg` — social avatar for round crops.

The lockups (E everyday, C signature) and the favicon are in `design/logo/masters/` and are added here once their final spacing is chosen.

Never recolour or outline the block, rotate the mark, close the gap, or use the old mark (small pale block).
