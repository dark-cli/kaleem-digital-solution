## Who we are

**EN.** We liberate data, modernize systems, and restore digital freedom. We specialize in extracting data from legacy systems and moving it into modern platforms, working with organizations that want to free their trapped data and build systems they truly own.

**AR.** نحرّر البيانات، ونحدّث الأنظمة، ونعيد إليك حريتك الرقمية. نتخصص في استخراج البيانات من الأنظمة القديمة ونقلها إلى منصات حديثة، ونعمل مع المؤسسات التي تريد تحرير بياناتها المهملة وبناء أنظمة تملكها حقاً.

## Our story

**EN.** Kaleem started with a simple question: how much valuable data is trapped in aging systems? How many Iraqi organizations hold years of archives they can't access or build on? Today we specialize in extracting that data, cleaning it, and rebuilding systems the modern way — safely and completely. Our projects show that moving from legacy to modern doesn't mean losing anything.

**AR.** بدأت كليم من سؤال بسيط: كم من البيانات القيّمة محبوس في أنظمة قديمة؟ وكم من المؤسسات العراقية تملك أرشيف سنوات طويلة لكنها لا تستطيع الوصول إليه أو البناء عليه؟ اليوم نتخصص في استخراج هذه البيانات وتنظيفها، وإعادة بناء الأنظمة بطريقة حديثة وآمنة. ومشاريعنا تثبت أن الانتقال من نظام قديم إلى نظام حديث ممكن دون أن تخسر شيئاً.

## Vision and mission

**EN.** Iraqi organizations should truly own their digital infrastructure — free from single-vendor lock-in, from trapped data, and from the fear of upgrading. By freeing data and investing in open infrastructure, we help Iraq build real digital independence.

**AR.** أن تملك المؤسسات العراقية أنظمتها الرقمية ملكية حقيقية — بلا ارتهان لمزوّد واحد، وبلا بيانات محبوسة، وبلا خوف من الترقية أو التطوير. عبر تحرير البيانات والاستثمار في بنية تحتية مفتوحة، نساعد العراق على بناء استقلالية رقمية حقيقية.

## Positioning statement

For Iraqi organisations whose data is trapped in old, locked or scattered systems, **Kaleem** is the partner that gets it out, rebuilds it on modern technology, and hands over every key — unlike typical providers, who keep the keys and make every future change depend on them.

## The name

كليم is Arabic for "the one you speak with": the person you talk to, and who talks back. We speak the language of old, tangled systems and translate them into clear, open ones — and we speak plainly with clients, in Arabic and English.

## Audiences

The same method serves every sector; only the systems differ.

| Segment | Typical problems | Where we usually start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Companies & utilities** | Money and records moving through many hands with no history; data spread across tools and vendors | Management systems with a permanent audit trail (see Deptmaster), networks and security |
| **Shops & retail** | Stock on paper or spreadsheets, an old POS nobody supports, theft | Store and POS systems, cameras |
| **Clinics & medical centres** | Old, slow sites nobody can log into; content split across languages | The Jailbreak, then one bilingual site (see Alimran) |
| **Government & NGOs** | Legacy systems nobody can maintain, vendor lock-in, strict data-ownership needs | Migration to open standards, documentation, training and handover |
