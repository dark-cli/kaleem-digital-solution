# Services and scope

Six services. One method. Each service owns one pastel and one line icon, used wherever it appears (cards, tags, buttons, illustrations); all six sit on one Services page with an anchor each (`/services/#jailbreak`, `#websites`, `#mobile`, `#systems`, `#networks`, `#consulting`). Every project ends the same way: your data, code and accounts in your name.

**Hosting sized to the job** — websites: free serverless → dedicated server → bare metal; systems: free cloud → dedicated server → on your premises.

## 01 · The Jailbreak — legacy system migration · تحرير الأنظمة

Colour: `butter`. Icon: the open box. Our signature service.

**For:** anyone stuck with an old website, app or management system: lost logins, data split between you and your provider, non-standard formats, a provider who makes leaving hard.

- Audit where your data actually lives.
- Extract it: content, media, customer and product records, settings.
- Clean and restructure it.
- Convert it to open standards (e.g. Markdown, CSV/JSON, SQL databases, standard media formats).
- Rebuild or re-host on modern, open-source tooling.
- Recover what belongs to you: old domains, Google Business profiles, social pages — with redirects so you keep your search traffic.

**AR.** نستخرج بياناتك من الأنظمة القديمة والمغلقة أينما كانت، ننظّفها ونرتّبها، ونحوّلها إلى معايير مفتوحة، ثم نعيد بناء النظام عليها ونسلّمك المفاتيح. ونستعيد لك ما يخصّك: النطاق القديم، وملف Google التجاري، وصفحات التواصل.

Colour: `sky`. Icon: a browser window.

## 04 · Management, store & desktop systems · أنظمة الإدارة والمتاجر

Colour: `mint`. Icon: a database.

- Inventory, point-of-sale and store systems; office, clinic and institution management; desktop applications.

## 05 · Networks & security · الشبكات والأمن

Colour: `apricot`. Icon: a camera.

- Surveillance cameras: supply, installation and configuration.

- Guest Wi-Fi portals that show your website to visitors and give them timed free access.
- Admin credentials and a network diagram handed over at the end.

## 06 · Consulting & training · الاستشارات والتدريب

## Engagement model and rates

These are Kaleem's standard rates (September 2026). Publish the engagement model on the website; whether to publish the numbers is a business decision.

| | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Work / implementation (setup, development, fixes, research) | $20 per hour |
| Consulting (advice, planning, calls, on-site visits) | $25 per hour |
| Extra staff training | $30 per hour (fixed) |
| Monthly plan (optional) | $100 per month for 10 hours of work and consulting; unused hours don't roll over |

- Every job starts with a written **work order**: scope, fixed price or hour cap, and timeline; 30–50% paid up front, the rest within 7 days of delivery.
- After a handover: **14 days** of free fixes and small edits (up to 10 hours), and **60 days** of free advice (up to 10 hours, including one training session).
- Third-party costs (hosting, domains, SMS/WhatsApp gateways, AI APIs, hardware) are paid by the client directly to the provider — never marked up.
- Prices in US dollars, payable in dollars or Iraqi dinars.
