# Method and values

This is the centre of the brand. Every service, every project and every page of the website shows it; the case studies prove it. The values and steps below are worded exactly as on kaleem.dev.

## The promise

**Liberate your data.** — **حرّر بياناتك.**

We get your data out of old, locked systems and rebuild it on modern tech that you own. From stuck to free, in five steps — and everything stays yours.

## Our values · قيمنا

Each value has its own pastel card on the About page.

1. **Real ownership** — Data and systems belong to the people who run them — never the other way around. — **ملكية حقيقية:** البيانات والأنظمة ملك لأصحابها — لا العكس.
2. **Vendor independence** — Open technology that never traps you in licenses or subscriptions. — **استقلالية عن المزوّد:** تقنيات مفتوحة لا تحبسك في رخصة أو اشتراك.
3. **Full transparency** — Everything documented and explained — no magic, no mystery. — **شفافية كاملة:** كل شيء موثّق ومشروح — لا ألغاز ولا غموض.
4. **Local competence** — An Iraqi team that understands your context and speaks your language. — **كفاءة محلية:** فريق عراقي يفهم ظروفك ويتحدث لغتك.
5. **Performance over promises** — Fast, reliable systems — or we don't consider them complete. — **أفعال لا وعود:** أنظمة سريعة وموثوقة — وإلا فهي لم تكتمل بعد.
6. **Training and support** — We make sure your team fully understands the system and can evolve it on its own. — **التدريب والدعم:** نتأكد أن فريقك يفهم النظام فهماً كاملاً ويستطيع تطويره بنفسه.

## From stuck to free · من عالق إلى حرّ

**Stuck with…** an old system you can't leave · data you can't reach · a slow, broken website.
**…we get you out in five steps**, the same every time, whatever we build. The track ends at a key: **Free**.

| 1 | **Audit** | Find where your data and systems really are. | التقييم |
| 2 | **Extract & clean** | Get it out, fix it, make it open. | الاستخراج والتنظيف |
| 3 | **Build** | On the right stack for the job. | البناء |

## Everything stays yours · كل شيء يبقى لك

Every account & password · The code, in your account · Domain in your name · Full documentation · Training for your team.
AR: كل الحسابات وكلمات المرور · الكود في حسابك · النطاق باسمك · توثيق كامل · تدريب لفريقك.

## What "handing over the keys" means

At the end of every project the client receives, in writing:

- Their **data and content**, in open formats.
- Their **domain**, registered in their name.
- Their **hosting and cloud accounts**, owned by them.
- The **code repository**, moved to their own account, with a **permanent, free licence** to use and modify it and to hire any other developer to maintain it. (Kaleem keeps the rights to its code and may reuse it elsewhere; the client's licence covers their own system.)
- **Admin credentials** for their devices and services, and short **documentation**.
- Removal of Kaleem's access, keys and tokens.

**AR.** في نهاية كل مشروع يستلم العميل: بياناته ومحتواه بصيغ مفتوحة، ونطاقه باسمه، وحسابات الاستضافة بملكيته، ومستودع الشيفرة في حسابه مع ترخيص دائم ومجاني باستعمالها وتعديلها والاستعانة بأي مطوّر آخر، وبيانات الدخول الإدارية، وتوثيقًا مختصرًا، ثم نزيل صلاحياتنا.

## What we don't do

- We don't hold accounts, domains or data hostage — ever.
- We don't promise search rankings, review counts or traffic numbers.
- We don't give legal or medical sign-off: privacy policies and specialist content are reviewed by the client's lawyer or expert.
- We don't produce media content ourselves; on request, we coordinate the professionals who do.
