Pill labels with a 1.5px ink outline, 28px tall, 13px/600; on paper by default, or filled with the pastel of the service they name.

- `tone`: a service id (`jailbreak` butter · `websites` sky · `mobile` peach · `systems` mint · `networks` apricot · `consulting` lilac) or any pastel. Leave it off for tech and topic chips ("Rust", "Arabic + English").
- Text is always `ink`. Use on case-study tiles and service cards to show what was involved. Never as buttons.
- Consumer provides the label (1–3 words) and `lang="ar"` for Arabic labels.
