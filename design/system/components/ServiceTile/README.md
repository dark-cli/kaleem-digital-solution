One service as a pastel sketch card: its 40px line icon, title, a one-line promise and optional inclusions.

- Six in a grid: 3 across on desktop, 2 below 1056px, 1 below 672px, 28px gaps, every row as tall as the tallest. Order: Jailbreak, Websites, Mobile, Systems, Networks, Consulting.
- Props: `service` (sets the pastel and the icon), `title`, `description` (one line), `items` (short noun phrases, under a dashed rule), `href` (the service's anchor on `/services/`), `icon={false}` to drop the icon, `dir`/`lang` for Arabic.
- With an `href` the whole card is the link and lifts up-left on hover and focus, pressing back on click.
- Text is `ink` on every pastel.
