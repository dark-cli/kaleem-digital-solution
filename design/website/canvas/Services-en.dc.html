01OUR SIGNATURE

The Jailbreak

Legacy system migration

We get your data out of old, locked systems, convert it to open standards, and hand you the keys.

WHO IT'S FOR

Anyone stuck with an old website, app or system: lost logins, data split between you and your provider, non-standard formats, a provider who makes leaving hard.

WHAT WE DO
  • — Find where your data actually lives
  • — Extract it: content, media, customer and product records, settings
  • — Clean and restructure it
  • — Convert it to open standards such as Markdown · CSV · JSON · SQL
  • — Rebuild or re-host on open-source tools
  • — Recover what's yours: old domains, Google Business profiles and social pages, with redirects that keep your search traffic
YOU RECEIVE

Your data in open formats, the new system, documentation, and every account in your name.

02

Websites & web apps

Fast, bilingual websites your team can edit, at the lowest possible hosting cost.

WHO IT'S FOR

Companies, shops, clinics and institutions that need a clear presence online.

WHAT WE DO
  • — Arabic and English in one site
  • — Static sites with no server to rent where possible; web apps with a backend when needed
  • — A content editor with live preview, so your team edits pages itself
  • — Speed, mobile layout and technical SEO
  • — On request: contact forms, FAQ, booking, AI chat assistants, customer portals, email on your own domain
YOU RECEIVE

The site, its domain and hosting account in your name, the code repository in your account, and editor training.

Mobile apps

Android and iOS apps for your customers or your team, published in your name.

WHO IT'S FOR

Anyone who needs an app for customers or staff, or has an old app that needs rebuilding.

WHAT WE DO
  • — Apps for ordering, bookings, catalogues, loyalty and internal use
  • — Upgrades and rebuilds of old apps, with their data migrated
  • — Published under your own developer accounts
YOU RECEIVE

The app published under your accounts, and the code repository in your account.

04

Management & store systems

Inventory, point-of-sale and management systems, in the cloud or on your premises.

WHO IT'S FOR

Shops, offices and institutions running on paper, spreadsheets or outdated software.

WHAT WE DO
  • — Inventory, point-of-sale and store systems
  • — Office, clinic and institution management; desktop applications
  • — Customer, patient or member records, with access rules agreed in writing
  • — Cloud or on-site servers — your choice, with the cost and data trade-offs explained
  • — Migration from spreadsheets, paper or old software
YOU RECEIVE

The system and its data, admin credentials, documentation and user training.

05

Networks & security

Surveillance cameras, security systems and organised networks, with every password in your hands.

WHO IT'S FOR

Offices, shops, clinics and facilities that need surveillance or a well-organised network.

WHAT WE DO
  • — Surveillance cameras: supply, installation and configuration
  • — Other security systems, such as access control, and their integration
  • — Separate staff and guest networks, Wi-Fi, routers and access points
  • — Guest Wi-Fi portals that show visitors your website and give timed free access
YOU RECEIVE

Admin credentials for every device, a network diagram and installation notes.

Consulting & training

We help you decide what to build, buy, keep or replace — and train your team.

WHAT WE DO
  • — Technology advice and planning
  • — Search and market analysis from your Search Console and Analytics data

  • — On request, coordinating media and branding teams for your campaigns

Written recommendations, a clear plan, and a team that knows how to run its systems.
