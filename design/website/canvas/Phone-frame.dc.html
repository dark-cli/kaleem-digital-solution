Component · PhoneFrame

The window, for phones.

Same family as our window widget: the hand-drawn outline and offset shadow, and a dark bezel that gets the sky shader just like the window's top bar. The screenshot sits in a rounded screen at its natural ratio (no cropping), so any phone screenshot drops straight in.

Anatomy
OUTLINE
2px ink, 36px corners, the same 4px offset shadow as cards and windows.
BEZEL
10px all round. Sky shader (A) or paper (B).
SPEAKER
A 54 × 6 pill in a 16px strip on top: enough to read as a phone, no fake status bar or notch cut-outs.
SCREEN
2px ink line, 24px corners, the screenshot at its own aspect ratio.
CLICKABLE
Lifts like the window widget on hover (same window-link motion).
