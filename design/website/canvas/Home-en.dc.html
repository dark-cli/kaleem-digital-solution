HOW WE WORK

Five steps from locked to free

The same method on every project — a website, an app, a store system or a camera network.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We map where your systems, accounts and data really are: your devices, your provider, old databases, spreadsheets.

  2. 02

    Extract & clean

    We get your data out, remove duplicates, fix formats and fill the gaps between Arabic and English.

  3. 03

    Rebuild on open standards

    We rebuild on modern, open tools, in formats any developer can read.

  4. 04

    Hand over the keys

    Domain, accounts and data in your name, the code repository in your account — then we remove our access.

  5. 05

    Support, then freedom

    Free support for a set period, then your choice: stay with us, a monthly plan, or another provider.
